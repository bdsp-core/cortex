\begin{abstract}
\noindent We summarize the mathematics and validation record of a closed pipeline that
(i) measures a rater's per-task perceptual skill and bias with a sequential Monte
Carlo (SMC) certification test, (ii) trains deficient tasks with an adaptive,
belief-driven training loop, and (iii) re-certifies with the same instrument. The
trainer's graduation is a stopping \emph{recommendation}, never a certification.
We present the observation model, the inference and decision rules, the learning
dynamics and their filters, the anytime-valid safety gates, the validation ladder
from simulation calibration to 125{,}860 real reads, and results from seven human
testers running the full stack. Seven decisions are put to the panel
(Section~\ref{sec:asks}). Every number cited is reproducible from a named script
in the accompanying tree; the full evidence trail is \texttt{docs/PROJECT\_MEMORY.md}.
\end{abstract}

\section{The pipeline}
The system is a closed loop over $K$ classification tasks. A \emph{certification
eval} measures a rater's latent traits with an adaptive test and issues
PASS/FAIL/REFER verdicts. Its final posterior, variance-inflated, seeds the
\emph{trainer}'s belief. The trainer runs a partially observed control loop:
per-task particle filters track the \emph{learning} rater, a mode-conditional
policy (bias-correction, skill-building, retention) selects items from a real
89k-item bank, and a deficiency-weighted scheduler interleaves tasks. When the
trainer's belief says a task is at bar, it \emph{recommends} stopping (decision
D18); certification authority stays with the eval instrument, which re-certifies
against the same bar the trainer trained to. Two coordinate systems are bridged
in exactly one module:
\begin{equation}
\sigma = e^{-\ell}, \qquad t = -\theta, \qquad
z = e^{\ell}(s+\theta) \equiv \frac{s-t}{\sigma},
\end{equation}
where $\ell$ is log-skill, $\sigma$ internal noise, $\theta$ (engine) or $t$
(trainer) the decision criterion, and $s$ the item's signed signal strength.

\section{Observation model}
Each trial is a yes/no judgment on a stimulus of signal strength $s$. The
response model is a probit-lapse signal-detection link,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:obs}
P(y=1 \mid \ell,\theta,s) \;=\; \lambda + (1-2\lambda)\,\Phi\!\big(e^{\ell}(s+\theta)\big),
\qquad \lambda = 0.025 \text{ (locked)},
\end{equation}
one likelihood definition shared by the calibration fitter, the eval engine, and
the trainer (fitted rater lapses are an order of magnitude below the lock).
The item signal is itself an estimate with known uncertainty
$s_{\mathrm{real}} \sim N(s, s_{\mathrm{sd}}^2)$, never observed directly; the
likelihood marginalizes it in closed form by attenuation,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:atten}
\mathbb{E}\big[\Phi(e^{\ell}(s_{\mathrm{real}}+\theta))\big]
= \Phi\!\left(\frac{e^{\ell}(s+\theta)}{\sqrt{1+(e^{\ell} s_{\mathrm{sd}})^2}}\right),
\end{equation}
verified by Monte Carlo to $10^{-4}$ and used consistently in every scorer,
selector, and filter. Skill is reported as a bias-free discrimination index,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{AUROC}(\ell) = \Phi\!\left(\sqrt{2}\,\big/\sqrt{e^{-2\ell}+1}\right),
\end{equation}
and the per-task mastery cut-scores $\ellstar_k \in [0.256, 0.534]$ correspond to
AUROC bars of roughly $0.87$--$0.89$.

\section{Certification: inference and decision rule}
The eval estimates the joint $2K$-vector $(\theta_{1:K}, \ell_{1:K})$. The prior
is zero-mean Gaussian with \emph{unit-diagonal correlation} matrices per block
(marginals $N(0,1)$; fitted cross-task correlations up to ${\approx}0.65$), so
evidence pools across correlated tasks. The posterior is carried by $N=600$
weighted particles: reweight on each answer; when
$\ESS = 1/\sum_i w_i^2$ falls below $N/2$, multinomially resample and apply
15 Metropolis--Hastings rejuvenation steps with Roberts--Gelman--Gilks proposal
scaling ($2.38/\sqrt{2K}$), sweeping the full history --- valid here because the
certified traits are \emph{static}. Item selection is Bayesian A-optimal: choose
the $(k, s)$ minimizing
\begin{equation}
\mathbb{E}[\text{loss}] = P(y{=}1)\, V^{+} + P(y{=}0)\, V^{-},
\qquad V^{\pm} = \textstyle\sum_k \Var^{\pm}(\theta_k) + \sum_k \Var^{\pm}(\ell_k),
\end{equation}
where $V^{\pm}$ are total posterior variances of the two hypothetical
one-step-ahead posteriors. The anytime verdict rule (AD6) computes, after every
trial and per task,
\begin{equation}
\pi_k = \sum_i w_i\, \mathbf{1}\{\ell_k^{(i)} > \ellstar_k\}, \qquad
\mathrm{mcse}_k = \sqrt{\pi_k(1-\pi_k)/\ESS}, \qquad
R_k = 1 - \frac{\Var_{\mathrm{post}}(\ell_k)}{\Var_{\mathrm{prior}}(\ell_k)},
\end{equation}
and resolves task $k$ only when $n_k \ge 20$ and $R_k \ge 0.30$ (an information
gate against prior-dominated verdicts), with
\begin{equation}
\text{PASS if } \pi_k - Z\,\mathrm{mcse}_k \ge 1-\alpha, \qquad
\text{FAIL if } \pi_k + Z\,\mathrm{mcse}_k \le \alpha, \qquad
(\alpha, Z) = (0.05,\, 2.0).
\end{equation}
The Monte Carlo buffer $Z\,\mathrm{mcse}$ guards the finite-particle estimate of
$\pi_k$; verdicts lock monotonically; unresolved tasks finalize as REFER
(borderline vs.\ uninformative, by whether the information gate ever opened).

\section{Training: dynamics, filters, and gates}
\subsection{Learner dynamics}
The trainer models the rater as a per-task hidden state $(\sigma_k, t_k)$ moving
under feedback. The criterion follows a soft Rescorla--Wagner update, gated to
feedback trials only, and log-skill relaxes toward a latent per-learner ceiling
$\sigma_\infty$ within a Gaussian difficulty window $w(\cdot)$ centered at the
85\%-correct placement:
\begin{align}
t' &= t + \alpha_t\big(\hat p(s_{\mathrm{real}}) - y^{*}\big) + \xi_t,\\
\log \sigma' &= \log \sigma + \alpha_\sigma\, f\, w(s_{\mathrm{real}})\big(\log
\sigma_\infty - \log \sigma\big) + \xi_\sigma,
\end{align}
where $y^{*}$ is the feedback label and both the response and the update depend
on the same unobserved $s_{\mathrm{real}}$. The Rescorla--Wagner form is the
constant-rate analogue of the cross-entropy gradient (re-derived and documented
as a behavioral postulate, not algebra). Rates are empirical-Bayes anchored to
1{,}158 real learning curves ($\alpha_t = 0.097$, $\alpha_\sigma = 0.047$),
deliberately conservative: over-estimated rates provably break filter coverage.

\subsection{Belief}
Each task carries a bootstrap particle filter (reweight, systematic resample,
propagate) with \emph{no} MH rejuvenation --- replaying history against a moving
state targets the wrong posterior. The transition kernel is the exact
conditional kernel, marginalizing the shared $s_{\mathrm{real}}$ by 21-node
Gauss--Hermite quadrature; this restores nominal coverage (criterion coverage at
the 90\% level: $0.63 \to 0.91$) and passes closed-loop simulation-based
calibration at every checkpoint. Static parameters cannot live on particles
(path degeneracy), so ceiling uncertainty is a Bayesian model average over a
$J=7$ grid of $\sigma_\infty$ hypotheses plus a ``does not learn'' stratum, with
prequential evidence weights. The resulting \emph{trainability}
$P(\ell_\infty > \ellstar \mid \text{data})$ is an honest posterior; it replaced
a real failure in which a filter that knows the ceiling clears the bar
eventually certified its own prior (30/30 false declarations on a static
below-bar learner; now 0--7/30 depending on margin). Real acquisition arrives as
\emph{discrete regime shifts at sitting boundaries}, so every session open
applies a $\sigma$-dominant state shock plus a fixed-share re-mix of the ceiling
weights,
\begin{equation}
\omega \leftarrow (1-\rho)\,\omega + \rho\,\omega_0, \qquad \rho = 0.10,
\end{equation}
the exact Bayes update for a hidden-Markov ceiling with per-boundary redraw
hazard $\rho$ (Herbster--Warmuth fixed-share).

\subsection{Anytime-valid gates}
Declarations are guarded by certification probes placed at the \emph{cut} state
$(\sigma^{*}, \hat t)$ --- Fisher-information-optimal for a scale parameter at
$\tilde z \approx \pm 1.35$ under the lapse link, and deliberately independent of
the believed skill, which the training placement tracks. Probe outcomes feed a
mixture of Hoeffding e-processes against every at-or-above-bar null,
\begin{equation}
e_\lambda = \exp\!\Big(\lambda \textstyle\sum_i (a_0 - c_i) - n\lambda^2/8\Big),
\qquad \lambda \in \{0.05, \dots, 0.8\},
\end{equation}
each a nonnegative supermartingale under its null, so by Ville's inequality the
probability of \emph{ever} blocking a truly at-bar learner is at most $\alpha$
with no multiple-looking correction (measured: type-I $0/200$; power $200/200$
against a $0.2$ accuracy deficit). The same construction yields a
contact-onboarding test (a Krichevsky--Trofimov predictor against a chance-band
null) and a stale-mastery tripwire in the retention layer.

\subsection{Zero-bias serving is derived, not assumed}
For the soft Rescorla--Wagner criterion under mirror-paired, label-alternating
items anchored at the true boundary with offset $a$ and effective noise
$\sigma_e = \sqrt{\sigma^2 + s_{\mathrm{sd}}^2}$,
\begin{equation}
\mathbb{E}[t_\infty] = 0, \qquad
\kappa = \frac{\alpha_t (1-2\lambda)\,\varphi(a/\sigma_e)}{\sigma_e}, \qquad
\Var_\infty = \frac{q_t^2 + \alpha_t^2 \Var[\hat p]}{\kappa(2-\kappa)}
+ \frac{(\alpha_t \bar\delta)^2}{(2-\kappa)^2},
\end{equation}
verified numerically to $\le 3\%$ across the parameter grid. Corollaries:
placement that tracks the estimated criterion has no restoring force (the
criterion random-walks, SD ${\approx}3$ vs.\ ${\approx}0.13$); strict label
alternation is load-bearing (i.i.d.\ balanced labels inject $\alpha_t^2/4$ of
diffusion, an order-of-magnitude worse floor); and ``zero bias'' is achievable
exactly to the band $\pm c\,\mathrm{SD}_\infty$, which is what the graduation
gate demands.

\subsection{Graduation semantics}
A declaration must survive a confirmation lifecycle (re-approach the bar after
the boundary hazard re-widens the belief) before it counts; confirmed
false-graduation (FG) is the priced safety quantity everywhere. Graduation
remains a recommendation: the eval instrument re-certifies, and its bar equals
the trainer's bar by construction.

\section{Validation ladder}
Six rungs, lowest to highest: (1) 299 script-style checks across 21 test files,
every calibrated constant pinned to the study that produced it; (2)
\emph{calibration} --- closed-loop simulation-based calibration of the training
filter nominal at every checkpoint, plus a paired open-loop kernel audit; (3)
\emph{misspecification envelope} --- a 12-member learner zoo (power-law,
plateau, drifting ceiling, heavy-tail link, asymmetric lapse, fatigue, careless,
anti-learner, static below-cut): training efficacy is robust across the zoo, and
graduation hardening brought adversarial FG to $1/90$ at the capstone; (4)
\emph{end-to-end safety} --- the re-certification backstop catches $75/75$
forced false graduations; (5) \emph{real-data validation} --- on 125{,}860 reads
by 699 users, the likelihood~\eqref{eq:obs} beats or matches alternatives
held-out; filter skill estimates track real accuracy (Spearman $\rho = 0.87$
binary task; $0.48$--$0.83$ multiclass); the fitted real link sits inside the
stress envelope; a cross-domain skill manifold is recovered ($\rho = 0.571$,
disattenuated $0.771$); dynamics priors are fitted from real learning curves;
(6) \emph{real humans on the full stack} --- Section~\ref{sec:sandbox}.

Field verification followed each fix: after the M21 corrections, 241 tester
trials produced zero label contradictions and four clean per-person states. The
bias-correction mode was validated on a real tester (criterion
$-0.48 \to -0.13$ with discrimination retained). The onboarding ramp turned the

trial 148 with 70\% completion, with healthy learners unharmed and careless
responders never certifying. Honest accounting: mean delivered per-trial
training value across the 14 real sessions was $0.48$ of the oracle policy's,
with belief-lag sessions at $0.15$--$0.17$ --- this number motivated the
boundary-hazard work and is the primary target of the pilot. The full suite is
green at 21 files / 299 checks; four consecutive checkpoints (M24--M27)
adjudicated every queued optimization successor end-to-end and none displaced
the shipped defaults, which we read as convergence of the current evidence base
and the reason iteration is now frozen under an explicit stopping rule.

\section{Operating characteristics and limitations}
A static learner parked $0.15$ \emph{below} the cut is declared ${\sim}30\%$ of
the time within 240 two-task trials: at that margin the per-probe accuracy
contrast is ${\approx}0.03$, so refutation needs $O(10^3)$ probes (Bernoulli
information bound, not gate inefficiency), and three candidate declaration
conjuncts were measured and rejected against it. The effective protection is
architectural: the confirmation lifecycle kills $5/6$ of these false
graduations (declared $6/20 \to$ confirmed $1/20$) and re-certification sits
above it ($75/75$). All FG claims are therefore priced at the \emph{confirmed}
level. Under the open-ended protocol with calendar-time forgetting, the median
simulated learner certifies ${\sim}2$ of 7 tasks in 40 sessions; consolidation
dynamics are a named pilot endpoint. The trainer certifies nothing; no existing
dataset contains closed-loop training, so the definitive methodology test is
the feedback-driven pilot. The onboarding ramp's pedagogical value is
deliberately unsimulated (simulating it would assume the conclusion) and is a
queued field check.

\section{Decisions requested of the panel}
\label{sec:asks}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Ratify recommendation-only graduation and the hardened
configuration} (exact kernel, mixture graduation, probes and e-gate) as the
port default.
\item \textbf{Acceptance semantics for narrow-margin domains}: is the
trailing-mean-skill graduation branch ($z = 1.645$, $W = 20$) acceptable as an
acceptance-testing semantic? (Disabled for mixture graduation.)
\item \textbf{Re-certification prior}: warm-start from the trainer posterior
(efficient) or a fresh prior (psychometric integrity)? We recommend fresh.
\item \textbf{Instrument caveats}: the claimed resolution gain of the v15
instrument is mostly bar recalibration under an anchoring convention, and
particle doubling does not fix posterior-$\ell$ coverage; the decisive coverage
run is port-gated. Comfortable?
\item \textbf{Confirmed-FG endpoint}: we propose all false-graduation claims,
including the pilot endpoint, move from declared to confirmed semantics
(measured $6/20 \to 1/20$). Please ratify before the pilot analysis plan
freezes.
\item \textbf{Pilot statistical analysis plan}: power target
$\alpha = 0.005$, power $0.9$, ${\sim}33$ per arm; consolidation-exponent
endpoint; decision-level outcomes only.
\item \textbf{Terminal-confirmation semantics}: should a \emph{confirmed} task
leave the training rotation for the scheduled re-certification layer? It buys
$+20$pp pair completion by design and moves post-confirmation regression
detection from the trainer (${\sim}50\%$ undetected in-run on the thin terminal
stream) to scheduled re-certs ($0\%$ when the trainer keeps re-polishing). We
recommend adoption for the pilot, which has scheduled re-certs; it is shipped
default-off pending your ratification.
\end{enumerate}
