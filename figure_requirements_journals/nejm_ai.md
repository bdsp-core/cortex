## Hard-verified (NEJM AI's own guidance)
- **Display items: 5 figures + tables combined** for an Original Article (also Datasets/Benchmarks, Review,
  Deployment Case Study; Methods=5; Policy Corner=2; Perspective=1; Editorial=0). 3000-word main text.
  *Verified by ≥3 independent snippets; distinct from flagship NEJM's 4.*
- **Word-count excludes** abstracts, **figure legends**, and table notes.
- Figures uploaded as **individual high-resolution files** (or PDFs), separate from the manuscript; if
  embedded, also uploaded separately.
- Each figure needs a **title + legend on the same page as the figure**, and a **source line**.
- **AI-created figures/illustrations/visualizations are NOT permitted.**
- **Permissions** for reproduced figures are the author's responsibility.
- **No colour charge / no APC** (NEJM AI has no author-pays model).

## Deferred to NEJM Group 2025 Technical Guidelines
- **Data visualizations** (bar/line/scatter/dot/survival/box/forest/flow/timeline/map) → **editable
  vector required: `.ai`, `.eps`, `.svg`.**
- **Photographic & medical images** (patient/surgical, histology, X-ray, CT/MRI, US, ECG) → **high-quality
  raster.**
- **Not accepted:** files exported directly from SPSS (`.sav`) / Stata (`.dta`). PDF accepted as a figure file.

## Practical submission rule
Submit **data figures as editable vector (PDF/EPS/SVG)**; photos/EEG images as high-quality raster
(≥300 dpi); 5 display items max; legend + source line on each; no AI-generated imagery; RGB; CVD-safe
palette (best practice, not stated). For un-stated technical specs, follow NEJM Group 2025 guidelines or
email the editorial office (AIeditors@nejm.org).
