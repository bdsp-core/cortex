# Epilepsia — figure / display-item specifications

**Publisher:** Wiley · official journal of the ILAE · ISSN 1528-1167 · **Researched:** 2026-06-03
**Status:** likely Paper 1 target (spike-feasibility methodology paper).

> **Access note:** Epilepsia's own author-guidelines page (`onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/15281167/
> homepage/forauthors.html`) returned HTTP 402; article-type limits + colour policy are from search snippets
> of that page. Technical specs are from Wiley's general figure guidance (2016 artwork PDF read in full +
> current `authors.wiley.com` figure-prep page, both fetched directly). No Epilepsia-specific technical
> override of Wiley's rules was found.

## File formats
- **Line art** (graphs, flowcharts, diagrams, scatter): **EPS or PDF** preferred ("when in doubt, submit PDF").
- **Images** (photos, MRI/US, drawings): **TIFF, PNG, or EPS** preferred.
- Post-acceptance also accepts GIF/JPG/TIF/PNG/WMF/DOC/PPT/PSD/AI/PS. Rule: **a figure with both line art
  and images follows the line-art rules** (format + DPI).

## Resolution (at final size)
- **Line art: 600–1000 dpi** (600 preferred at review; 600–1000 production).
- **Images/halftone: 300 dpi.**
- **Combination: governed by the line-art rule → 600 dpi+** (legacy Wiley guide states 600 for combination).
- Checklist phrasing: "300–600 DPI" general range; must be legible at 80 mm / 1800 px unmagnified.
- ⚠ The often-cited "1200 dpi line art" is **not** current Wiley standard (cap is 1000 dpi).

## Dimensions
- **Small / single column: 80 mm (≈3.15″)** (Wiley Graphics FAQ: 8.5 cm ≈3.35″). **Min 1800 px wide.**
- **Large / full width: 180 mm (≈7.09″)** (FAQ: 17.8 cm ≈7.01″). **Min 1800 px wide.**
- Max height: **not stated** for main Epilepsia (Epilepsia *Open* search summary cited ~22.5 cm / 9″).

## Fonts
- **Sans-serif, preferably Helvetica or Arial.** **Minimum 8 pt at final print size** (scale up if the
  figure will be reduced). Embed fonts in EPS (or outline text to paths).
- Calibri mentioned as acceptable for Epilepsia *Open* labels (not confirmed for main journal).

## Colour
- **RGB for online; CMYK for print** (Wiley converts RGB→CMYK). Submit RGB.
- **No charge for colour figures** — Epilepsia *encourages* colour (high-confidence from search snippets;
  verify at submission). Distinguishes Epilepsia from Wiley journals that charge ~£350/first colour page.
- **CVD:** colour-blind-friendly palette **recommended** (Epilepsia *Open* prefers its own palette but it is
  not mandatory). No specific CVD hex set retrievable.

## Display items by article type (from search snippets of the live guidelines)
| Type | Words | Abstract | Refs | **Figures+Tables** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Full-Length Research Article | 4,000 | ≤300 structured | ≤50 | **≤6 combined** |
| Brief Communication | 1,800 | ≤200 | ≤18 | **≤2 combined** |
| Review Article | 5,000 | — | ≤100 | "ample" (no fixed cap stated) |

## Panel labels / line weight / file size
- **Panel labels: lowercase a, b, c…, 12 pt sans-serif (Arial/Helvetica), top-left, consistent.**
- **Minimum line weight: 0.5 pt (0.2 mm)** (Wiley artwork guide; hairlines discouraged).
- **File size: <10 MB each, <500 MB total** (zipped article). Use TIFF LZW compression.

## Legends / permissions / integrity
- Legends in a **separate "Figure Legends" section after the reference list** (not embedded in the image);
  "Figure N" in bold; explain all symbols/abbreviations. Word limit not specified.
- Reproduced material needs written permission + credit (author, date, title, publisher) in the legend.
- Integrity: no enhancing/obscuring/moving/removing features; brightness/contrast only uniformly + to
  controls; non-linear adjustments disclosed in legend; originals provided on request; gels/blots have
  extra rules (rarely relevant to EEG figures).

## Gaps / not specified
Epilepsia-specific exact column widths/height/font-min/line-weight (defaults to Wiley general); CVD palette
hex; combination-DPI as a distinct category in the 2016 PDF; legend word limits; per-figure colour fee
(none for Epilepsia). The Jan-2025 Wiley figure PDF (`basic-figure-requirements-1737125359497.pdf`) was
paywalled but search-summarized as confirming 600/300 dpi, 80–180 mm, <10 MB.
